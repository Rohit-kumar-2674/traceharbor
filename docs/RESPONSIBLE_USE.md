# Responsible investigation

TraceHarbor supports public-interest research, training and careful organisation of
information. Calling an investigation “public service” does not itself establish
authority to collect, identify, track or disclose information about a person.

## Working principles

1. Define a specific, proportionate purpose and document the source/authority.
2. Collect the minimum relevant information. Prefer consented or non-personal material.
3. Keep facts, machine output, disputed information and analyst hypotheses separate.
4. Record contradictory evidence and uncertainty. Correct mistakes with a linked note.
5. Treat editable metadata, OCR and candidate usernames as leads, not proof.
6. Restrict access and review exports for private information before sharing.
7. Follow applicable procedures for preservation, retention and deletion.

## Excluded capabilities

No identification of private people from faces, covert tracking, private-account
access, credential acquisition, access-control bypasses, surveillance integrations,
automatic allegations or people-ranking/risk-scoring systems are included.

## Police and public-service evaluation

Use synthetic training cases first. Do not enter live victim/witness/suspect data in
this release merely because the UI appears professional. An independent assessment
must determine whether controls and accuracy are suitable for the intended workflow.
Human investigators retain responsibility for collection, corroboration and review;
the tool does not make investigative or legal decisions.

Never upload case data to this repository, GitHub issues or public demonstrations.
